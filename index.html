About Hsiao Financial and Retirement, LLC

Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Hsiao Financial and Retirement, LLC is a Fee-Only Registered Investment Advisory firm dedicated to providing accessible, personalized, and technology-enabled wealth guidance.

Our Mission

To empower individuals, executives, and organizations across the San Francisco Bay Area with accessible, high-touch, and technology-driven financial guidance, ensuring long-term financial independence and secure retirement transitions.

Who We Serve

We serve solo professionals, private investors, small-to-medium business owners, corporate executives, human resources directors, program managers, and executive leadership (presidents and CEOs) aged 18 and older throughout Northern California.

Our Tech & Media Ecosystem

Backed by $1,000,000 in dedicated startup capital, our modern platform combines direct 1-on-1 advisor relationships with an educational podcast, an interactive client portal, and a 24/7 AI Financial Assistant for seamless baseline analysis.

Our Core Offerings

Comprehensive retirement and financial consulting services engineered to solve complex accumulation and distribution challenges.

Retirement Planning & FIRE Strategy

Customized long-term wealth accumulation and decumulation models tailored for early retirement, financial independence, and sustainable lifestyle planning.

401(k) & Roth IRA Optimization

Strategic 401(k) rollover guidance, Roth IRA conversion ladders, and tax-efficient portfolio restructuring designed to preserve equity compensation.

Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) Allocation

Specialized fund distribution and allocation models crafted specifically for federal employees, civil service staff, and government contractors.

Corporate HR & Business Consulting

Executive retirement workshops, key-person financial planning, key executive equity consulting, and customized group retirement plan structuring for employers.

1-on-1 Fiduciary Advisory

Personalized, direct consultation sessions with local wealth advisors to address unique life transitions, equity exercises, and legacy planning goals.

AI Financial Assistant Portal

Interactive 24/7 digital portal featuring automated preliminary portfolio intake, retirement gap modeling, and immediate baseline projection assessments.
